I am a graduate from **Universitas Lampung** with a strong passion for **Visual-Centric Robotics Perception** and **Computer Vision**. My ultimate goal is to contribute to the field as a **Research Scientist**, developing intelligent, vision-driven autonomous systems that can effectively perceive and interact with complex physical environments.

---

## 🔬 Research Interests

My core research focus revolves around enabling robots to "see" and comprehend the world using visual data. Key areas of interest include:

* **Visual SLAM & Spatial AI:** Simultaneous Localization and Mapping using monocular, stereo, or RGB-D cameras.
* **3D Perception & Reconstruction:** 3D object detection, semantic segmentation, and point cloud processing.
* **Learning-based Perception:** Integrating deep learning techniques with classical geometric computer vision.
* **Autonomous Navigation:** Vision-based state estimation and spatial awareness for mobile robots and autonomous agents.
