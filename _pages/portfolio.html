Evidence standard

A project page should become a polished research page only after the underlying work supports it. The minimum evidence gate is: functioning baseline, deterministic evaluation, quantitative results, a defensible extension or robustness study, failure analysis, reproducibility metadata, and an explanation I can defend orally.
