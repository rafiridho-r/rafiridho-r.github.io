**Status:** Planned  
**Area:** Learned 3D Perception

This project reproduces a scaled **PointPillars** pipeline for 3D object detection and then studies its behaviour under controlled degradation of point-cloud observations.

The main question is:

> How does pillar-based 3D detection degrade under controlled point-cloud corruption and reduced observation quality?

The study will focus on candidate-owned pillarisation logic, clean-performance verification, robustness experiments, compute-aware evaluation, and failure analysis.

Results, code, and quantitative robustness curves will be added after the project reaches the corresponding evidence milestone.
