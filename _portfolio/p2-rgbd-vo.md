**Status:** Planned  
**Area:** Camera Geometry / Ego-Motion

This project studies **RGB-D visual odometry in dynamic scenes**, with emphasis on camera geometry, SE(3), robust correspondence handling, pose estimation, and trajectory evaluation.

The main question is:

> How robustly can camera motion be estimated when parts of the observed scene are independently moving?

The scope is deliberately limited to a compact visual-odometry pipeline rather than a full SLAM system. Evaluation will focus on controlled experiments, ATE/RPE, dynamic-feature rejection, and failure analysis.

Results and implementation details will be added after the project reaches the corresponding evidence milestone.
