Robust RGB-D Visual Odometry in Dynamic Scenes

Camera geometry and ego-motion estimation under independently moving scene content.

Rafi Ridho Ramadhan

Independent Research Portfolio · Computer Vision / Robotics Perception

Research indexGitHub
TL;DR. Build a compact RGB-D visual-odometry pipeline and study how robust correspondence handling changes pose estimation when dynamic observations contaminate the scene.

Overview

How robustly can camera motion be estimated when parts of the observed scene are independently moving?

This project targets frame-to-frame / short-window RGB-D visual odometry, not a full SLAM system. The emphasis is on geometry, SE(3), correspondence quality, robust pose estimation, and trajectory error.

Evaluation plan

Geometry tests

Unit-test projection/back-projection, transform direction, SE(3) composition, and residual definitions.

Trajectory error

Evaluate ATE/RPE on a small set of RGB-D sequences with fixed alignment and reporting conventions.

Dynamic scenes

Compare baseline and robust estimation under sequences or masks containing independent motion.

Failure analysis

Inspect low texture, blur, depth holes, dynamic occlusion, and poor correspondence regimes.
